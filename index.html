Transactions Ledger

Goals Are Special Groups (Goal = Yes): Every transaction has a Group :: Category assignment. Standard budget Groups and 🎯 Goal Groups both appear in the Group dropdown. A Goal Group can have 0, 1, or many child Categories (if 0, select Direct to Goal in the Category dropdown).

Refunds & Reimbursements (Not Income): Retail returns (Amazon, Walmart, Menards) and peer reimbursements (Venmo/PayPal splits) should be entered as negative amounts (-$) under the same expense Group & Category as the original purchase. This offsets your category spending rather than inflating Income.

Transfers & Healing: Moving money between tracked accounts (like paying a credit card from checking) belongs in a 'Transfer' group. To split a purchase (e.g., at Menards) between a normal household category and a 🎯 Goal project, click the ✂ Split icon.

Color Guide: Red = Expenses (+$ Out)  |  Green = True Income or Negative Expense Refunds/Offsets (-$)  |  Grey = Transfers (Internal Move)
