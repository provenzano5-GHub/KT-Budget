Install App to Secure Data

Mobile operating systems will silently delete browser data to free up space. To prevent your financial data from being wiped, you must install this app to your Home Screen or Desktop.

iOS (Safari): Tap the Share icon at the bottom, scroll down, and tap Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): Tap the 3-dot menu, then tap Add to Home screen.

Desktop: Click the monitor/install icon in the address bar, or save as a window shortcut.

How to Account for Next Month's Actuals

Method 1: The Native Bulk-Import Wizard (Recommended)

This app is fully equipped to parse raw bank files directly on your device, ensuring privacy and speed.

  • Log into your bank and click "Download Activity as CSV".
  • In this app, click the 📥 Data Importer tab.
  • Drag and drop your raw bank CSV file into the designated zone.
  • The engine will instantly parse the file, flip expenses to positive values, and attempt to auto-guess the Categories based on your past ledger entries.

Method 2: AI Bulk Categorization (Alternative)

Use a strict prompt in Gemini or ChatGPT to categorize massive batches of new merchants before importing.

Zero-to-One: AI Data Prep Engine

Instead of building your accounts, groups, and categories manually, use an LLM to design your bespoke relational architecture based on your actual spending habits.

  • Download your raw bank/credit card CSVs.
  • Click the button below to copy the KTBudget Architecture Generator Prompt.
  • Paste the prompt and your raw CSV text into the AI.
  • The AI will output a complete .json file representing your tailored database.
  • Save that text as a .json file, use the Wipe All Data & Reset button on the Admin tab, and then Restore from File to load your custom setup!
